# Word data

`words.js` and `forms.json` are generated by `tools/build-words.py` — do not edit them by hand.

## Sources and licenses

- **Words, CEFR levels (A1–C2), part of speech, frequency rank:**
  [KELLY word-list Greek](https://inventory.clarin.gr/lcr/741), Institute for Language and Speech
  Processing (ILSP) / Athena Research Center, part of the EU KELLY project
  (Kilgarriff et al. 2014, *Corpus-based vocabulary lists for language learners for nine languages*,
  Language Resources and Evaluation 48:121–163). License: **CC BY-NC 4.0** (non-commercial use only).
  The levels were derived mainly from corpus frequency, not set by hand.
- **English glosses, articles, romanization, inflected forms (`forms.json`):** English Wiktionary via the
  [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Greek/) extract. License: **CC BY-SA**.
- **About 1000 glosses** that Wiktionary lacks, plus a few overrides: hand-written,
  in `tools/glosses-extra.tsv`.

Because of the KELLY license, the app (or at least this word list) may not be used commercially.

## Known quirks of the KELLY list

- 11 entries had no level and are counted as C2; C2 also holds ~2000 words without a frequency rank.
- `αυτονομία` was listed at rank 11 / A1 (clearly an error) and is moved to C1 (`FIXES` in the build script).
- Some levels look odd (e.g. `χωράφι` in A1) — that is how the frequency-based list has them.
